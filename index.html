About Our Academy

Forming Strong Foundations for Lifelong Success

At Queens Blessed Standard Academy, our focused mission is to provide an exemplary, well-rounded foundational education that nurtures each pupil academically, morally, and spiritually.

By focusing intentionally on child-centered learning and solid basic education, we ensure each student receives the close personal attention, cognitive enrichment, and moral guidance needed to excel.

  • Montessori & Phonics Foundations: Fluent reading, comprehension, and numeracy established right from early childhood.
  • Primary Cognitive Mastery: Strong problem-solving skills in Mathematics, Quantitative Reasoning, English, and Basic Sciences.
  • Comprehensive Exam Preparation: Thorough academic training that equips students with the mastery to write and pass any external exams.
