Ages 2 – 5 Years

Kindergarten & Nursery School

In our Early Childhood development wing, our goal is to awaken joy, natural curiosity, and early linguistic confidence in every child. We combine Montessori sensory apparatus with Jolly Phonics and interactive play.

Jolly Phonics

Multi-sensory sounds and actions teaching pupils to read words independently before age 4.

Montessori Math

Handling tangible counting beads, geometric puzzles, and spatial recognition blocks.

  • Nurturing caregivers & registered pediatric first-aid matrons.
  • Low teacher-to-child ratio for individual guidance.
  • Soft indoor playground and safe outdoor play area.

Basic 1 to 6 (Ages 5 – 11)

Primary School Education

Our primary curriculum transitions learners from foundational sensory play into rigorous analytical thought, reading comprehension, creative writing, quantitative reasoning, and science exploration.

Quantitative Reasoning

Building logical problem-solving and rapid mental arithmetic abilities.

ICT & Computer Science

Keyboarding, creative software, and introduction to computer thinking.

  • Regular continuous assessments with detailed termly progress reports.
  • Intensive Common Entrance Examination drills starting in Basic 5.
  • Active participation in science fairs, spelling bees, and mathematics olympiads.

JSS 1 to 3 (Basic 7 – 9)

Junior Secondary School (JSS)

Our Junior Secondary school program is the bridge between primary innocence and senior secondary leadership. We maintain high discipline and intensive preparation for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Integrated Science

Hands-on exposure to practical physics, chemistry, and biology fundamentals.

Pre-Vocational Studies

Business studies, basic technology, home economics, and entrepreneurship.

  • 100% distinction pass rate in State and National BECE exams.
  • Specialized subject teachers for every junior secondary discipline.
  • Debate club, leadership prefectship, and career counseling sessions.
